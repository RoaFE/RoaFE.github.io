    Msc Advanced Computing Science at Strathclyde

    In 2020 I studied a post graduate course in Advanced Computing Science at the University of Strathclyde achieving my Masters and graduating with a Distinction. For my Master's project and dissertation, I invesitgated the feasibility of detecting Glaciers with a Recurrent Neural Network (RNN) using commercial satellite images.

    Bsc(hons) Computer Games Technology At Abertay

    After leaving high school I studied Computer Games Technology at Abertay University where I graduated with a 1st, the highest grade possible in Scotland for a Bachelor with Honours, in 2020. Here I learned and gained experience with C++, OpenGL, Direct-X, SFML, Python, asynchronous programming, Applied Mathematics, Mathematics in general and Animation programming and Augmented Reality. Some of the projects related to my time at University can be found in the projects page.
